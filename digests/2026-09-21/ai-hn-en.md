### 3. Community Sentiment Signal
The current mood on HN is characterized by a complex blend of high engagement and rising skepticism. The most active topics, such as the debate over AI-generated posters and the "Kool-Aid" threads, suggest a community that is no longer in a phase of blind excitement. Instead, there is a strong push for critical analysis of AI's utility in creative and professional domains.

There is a clear point of controversy regarding privacy and labor. The ChatGPT ad collector story and the "theft of labor" legal briefs indicate that users are more concerned with the corporate and ethical side of AI development than they were in previous cycles. The shift in focus from "what can the model do" to "at what cost" is notable. The "System One Models" post, despite its high score, is being read through this lens—users are eager for progress but are hyper-aware of the security vulnerabilities (OpenAI breach) and the "AI slop" that is currently permeating the web.
