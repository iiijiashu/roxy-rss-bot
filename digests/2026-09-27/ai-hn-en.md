## Community Sentiment Signal
Today's HN community is defined by a transition from general AI enthusiasm to a more defensive and security-focused posture. The most active discussions, driven by high comment counts (up to 1,126), are centered on OpenAI and Anthropic releases, but the tone has shifted from "how to use" to "how to secure." The highest volume of debate (854 comments) surrounds the Anthropic court ruling, reflecting deep anxiety over the intersection of AI companies and national security. 

There is a clear consensus forming that AI agents are becoming primary vectors for cyberattacks, evidenced by the top-ranked post about agents hacking Hugging Face. On a cultural level, the community is grappling with the "value of human work" in the face of capable models, leading to high engagement in educational and philosophical threads. Compared to previous cycles, the focus has moved away from "can LLMs do X" to "what do we do when LLMs do X on their own," signaling a maturation in the developer community's understanding of agentic risk.
