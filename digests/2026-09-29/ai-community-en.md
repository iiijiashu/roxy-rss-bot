## Community Pulse
The developer community is currently navigating the tension between AI hype and practical engineering. A dominant theme is "agent skepticism," with developers realizing that many AI agents are expensive, fragile wrappers around simple logic, leading to calls for better architectural decisions and cost management. Simultaneously, there is anxiety about skill atrophy, where AI's ability to fix bugs without human comprehension is viewed as a danger to long-term competence. Infrastructure and governance are moving to the forefront, with discussions on AI gateways, vector database necessity, and homomorphic encryption highlighting that AI development is becoming an engineering and security discipline rather than just a model-prompting one. Communities are also seeing a cultural shift, with vocal movements questioning major tech players like Google and demanding transparency from AI labs.
