## 3. Trend Signal Analysis
The dominant trend today is the **explosive growth of Agent Skills as a reusable capability layer**. Google's official skills repo (+528), Addy Osmani's production-grade skills (+680), and PrimeIntellect's self-improving agent (+2,356) all launched or surged today, signaling that the community is moving beyond monolithic agents toward composable, shareable skill units. Simultaneously, **code-level RAG** (code-graph-rag +96, graphify at 104k) and **agent performance optimization** (ECC 239k, headroom 65k) reflect a maturing ecosystem where developers care less about "can it agent" and more about "is it reliable and efficient." The emergence of vectorless RAG approaches (graphify, PageIndex) also hints at growing fatigue with vector-store overhead for codebase understanding. Meanwhile, established RAG leaders (dify, open-webui, langchain) maintain their dominance, suggesting the space is consolidating around a few key platforms rather than fragmenting.
