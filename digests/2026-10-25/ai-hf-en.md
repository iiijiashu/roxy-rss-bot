3. **Ecosystem Signal**
The ecosystem is showing a distinct bifurcation between massive proprietary-scale open weights and a thriving local quantization community. Model families like Qwen, DeepSeek, and Lightricks are gaining momentum, with users actively stripping safety filters and applying advanced quantization (FP8, GGUF, mixed-precision) to fit models onto consumer hardware. This indicates a strong consumer preference for private, uncensored, and easily deployable AI. While foundation models are being released with open licenses, the bulk of the engagement is in the secondary market of fine-tunes. The video generation space is seeing explosive adoption, particularly for single-file diffusion models that integrate directly into popular creative tools like ComfyUI.
