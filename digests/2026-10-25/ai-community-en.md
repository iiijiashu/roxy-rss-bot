4. **Community Pulse**
The AI developer community is entering a phase of hardening and pragmatism. While early excitement focused on capabilities, today's discourse is dominated by the operational friction of integration: how to secure agent workflows, prevent API key leaks, and manage the exponential token costs of quadratic attention in long-running sessions. RAG systems are moving past simple retrieval into complex, version-controlled knowledge graphs, revealing that even "correct" results are often factually outdated without strict temporal validation. Furthermore, a counter-cultural movement is emerging around code quality. Developers are actively building and deploying "anti-slop" linters to quantify and reject the degrading quality of AI-generated code. Both platforms heavily favor low-resource local engineering, showing a distinct trend of bringing massive models down to laptop GPUs and CPU nodes through aggressive quantization.
