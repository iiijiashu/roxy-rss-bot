## Community Pulse
Developers are increasingly concerned with the "hidden costs" of AI, whether that means $40,000/year from a single `console.log`, 92,000 tokens burned by MCP servers, or the security risks of indirect prompt injection. The "Touch Grass" Hacktoberfest theme has pushed the community to build practical, nature-integrated tools, signaling a desire for AI to enhance human life rather than just automate code. A major friction point is observability: many developers are discovering that green traces and successful benchmarks don't guarantee an agent actually did useful work. The community is shifting from "can we build it?" to "how do we verify it works without breaking it?" and "how do we do this locally?"
