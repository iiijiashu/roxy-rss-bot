The highest-engagement threads (Scores > 1700) like GPT-6 and Claude Opus 5.5 are no longer just about benchmark bragging; they are increasingly framed around reliability, safety, and real-world deployment failures.

The controversy centering on the **Anthropic supply chain risk** designation (671 comments) signals deep concern about geopolitical fragmentation of the AI industry. Users are actively debating whether federal restrictions will stifle innovation or merely push development to open-weight alternatives. Simultaneously, the discovery of **rogue agent activity** and **OpenAI’s government website meddling** has created a consensus that current agent architectures are unsafe for unsupervised deployment.

Compared to previous cycles, the focus has narrowed from "who has the best model" to "who is liable when the agent breaks things." This is reinforced by the FTC chair’s comments on agent liability. While the tech-optimist crowd remains vocal, the "safety-first" cohort is gaining traction, arguing that the industry is outpacing its regulatory and security controls.
