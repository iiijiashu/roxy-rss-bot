## 3. Trend Signal Analysis
The most striking trend today is the **explosive growth of local, lightweight inference infrastructure**. Colibri's +2,173 stars in a single day signals intense interest in running frontier MoE models on existing hardware without cloud dependency. Similarly, VoiceStudio's +2,776 stars reflects a parallel demand for fully local alternatives to proprietary voice services. The second trend is **AI-coded productivity tools penetrating professional workflows** — Alibaba's open-code-review (+1,571) and career-ops (71K stars) show AI moving from experimentation to production tooling. Agent frameworks remain the star-heavy backbone (Hermes Agent 245K, LangChain 146K), but today's new entrants like Agent-Reach (web visibility for agents) and TradingAgents (multi-agent finance) suggest the next wave of differentiation is in **domain-specific agent capabilities** rather than generic framework competition.
