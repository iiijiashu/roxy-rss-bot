### Community Pulse
The most significant theme across both platforms is the security and safety of autonomous agents. Developers are increasingly worried about "prompt injection" attacks where malicious code in repositories can manipulate AI agents, as well as the risks of leaked credentials in agentic workflows. This is paired with a broader anxiety about the "worse engineer" phenomenon, where reliance on AI may erode fundamental coding skills. Practical concerns are shifting toward operational details like token efficiency, context management, and the reliability of AI-generated tests. Emerging patterns include the adoption of `AGENTS.md` files to standardize agent behavior and the use of multiple LLMs to cross-check ("argue") for accuracy. There is a strong movement toward making AI development more deterministic and secure, moving away from purely probabilistic outputs.
