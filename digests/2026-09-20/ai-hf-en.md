### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The model ecosystem is showing a strong dual trend: frontier multimodal releases (like DeepSeek V4.1-Flash and MiniMax H3) are driving massive download volumes, while the quantization community (unsloth, ISTA-DASLab, prism-ml) is simultaneously optimizing these models for local deployment. Open-weight models remain dominant, with Qwen3.8-27B acting as the central pivot point for community fine-tuning and format conversion. Notably, there is a surge in efficiency-focused engineering, such as ternary quantizations and specialized MoE architectures like Edge0-35B-A3B, indicating that the market is prioritizing high performance on constrained, edge hardware alongside top-tier cloud capabilities.
