### 3. Community Sentiment Signal
Today’s sentiment is characterized by a heightened sense of urgency regarding "AI in the real world," moving beyond theoretical benchmarks to practical failures and legal liabilities. The highest engagement (scores 496-713) belongs to threads involving military intelligence errors, OpenAI’s legal verticals, and the interoperability of coding agents. A clear point of consensus is that the "hallucination" problem is becoming a critical failure mode in high-stakes sectors, with the US military incident serving as the primary evidence of this gap in safety. Conversely, there is a sharp divide between "pragmatists" who see the shift to `AGENTS.md` as a solution to fragmentation and "purists" who argue that writing with LLMs is fundamentally destructive to human creative skill. Compared to the previous cycle, the focus has shifted from model performance (benchmarks) to operational security, legal risk, and societal integration. The mood is less celebratory of new releases and more wary of the side effects of rapid deployment.
